## The challenge

To find out whether a site or project was worth pursuing, affordable housing organizations had to hire firms like a national general contractor to run a feasibility assessment. Each one took more than 12 months and cost over $100,000, which limited how many projects they could explore at all.

## What the team did

The team sold the project and built an automated feasibility platform for the contractor.

- Turned the year-long consulting assessment into an automated analysis that returns an answer in minutes
- Made it free for housing organizations, so any site or project can be checked
- Opened it to the wider market, where it became the contractor's North American lead-generation engine

## The outcome

Assessments went from more than 12 months and $100K+ each to minutes, at no cost to housing organizations. The platform ranked first nationally in a $5M government technology grant review.
