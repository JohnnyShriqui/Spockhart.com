## The situation

A national general contractor had years of expertise in judging which development opportunities were worth pursuing, but no way to offer it at scale to the builders, developers and organizations in a new market.

## What we did

We turned that expertise into a digital platform anyone in the market could use. Every assessment became a lead, giving the company a new way to start conversations with customers and win business.

## The result

Access to a $1B+ market, and a lead-generation engine reaching builders, developers and organizations across North America.
