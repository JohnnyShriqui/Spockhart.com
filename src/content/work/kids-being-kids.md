## Who they are

Kids Being Kids was founded in 2022 by two students. In their first seven months they moved more than $10,000 of gear and shoes to kids, with no budget and no staff. Their line: “We Share, Everyone Plays.”

## The challenge

Collecting gear was never the hard part. Getting the right gear to the right kid in the right week is.

- Registration is funded, the gear isn’t. In London, a child’s fees can be covered, but not equipment, and a beginner hockey set runs $300 to $600 new.
- Gear arrives months before kids need it. Outgrown skates come free in April and aren’t needed until fall; gloves come free in August and wait for spring.
- Every step runs on the founders. Drives, sorting and shoe orders run on forms, spreadsheets and spare time, so every drive starts from zero.

## The insight

Start with the kid, not the garage. A referral comes first: a named kid, a sport, their sizes and a first-practice date. Gear is matched from the shelf, and only what’s missing gets collected. Kids Being Kids already works this way for shoe orders. KitMatch opens it to every sport and every partner program.

## What we’re building

KitMatch, on low-cost, off-the-shelf tools a volunteer can run, so nothing breaks when we step back.

- A referral form partner staff fill in for each kid in about two minutes
- A gear log: every item labeled by sport, size and condition, with helmets safety-checked at intake
- A match that runs every night against open referrals
- A gap list of what’s short, by sport, size and date needed, which aims the next drive
- A pick list for every kit, and a pickup time for the partner
- A note to every donor on where their gear went, and a live impact page for sponsors

## How it pays for itself

Team Kit Days. A company sponsors one partner program’s kits for the season, and its team spends a half day packing them, each kit carrying a kid’s first name. The sponsor gets its logo on the kit bags and a live page with the season’s numbers. The money buys only what donations can’t cover, like new helmets, kit bags, storage and transport.
