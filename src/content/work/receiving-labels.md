## The challenge

Incoming material could only be formally received once it had been recorded at a designated location. This created a bottleneck: when that location could not process incoming material, deliveries accumulated on-site, delaying inventory intake and disrupting operations.

## What the team did

The team redesigned the receiving process to eliminate the single-location bottleneck.

- Identified the centralized receiving process as the source of delays
- Redesigned the workflow to allow material to be recorded at its point of arrival
- Eliminated the dependency on a single receiving location

## The outcome

Receiving delays were reduced from approximately two hours to minutes. Incoming material could now be recorded upon arrival, eliminating the previous bottleneck and improving the flow of materials across the operation.
