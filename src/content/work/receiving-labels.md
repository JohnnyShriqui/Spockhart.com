## The challenge

At a contract manufacturer, incoming material could not be received until it was marked, and only one site could do it. Whenever the other site came up short, material sat waiting on the floor and the team lost hours.

## What the team did

- Traced the delays to a single point of failure in how material was received
- Redesigned the receiving process so material is marked where it arrives
- Removed the dependency on a single site

## The outcome

The wait went from hours to minutes. Incoming material is now received and put away as it arrives, and receiving no longer depends on one site.
