## The situation

A contract packaging manufacturer needed to improve production efficiency and quality control across its assembly floor.

## What we did

We redesigned the staff and assembly layout and introduced a new inspection process for incoming materials and finished goods.

The changes improved production enough to exceed daily targets an average of three days per week. The redesigned layout was subsequently adopted as standard operating procedure.

## The result

A more efficient production floor, stronger quality controls, and improvements that became part of everyday operations.
